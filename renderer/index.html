One press

sets the numbers that matter

The preset is the plan that survived measurement on the live market, written in the same net-of-everything percentages the rules use. It is deliberately a one-slot, patient desk: a small wallet pays most of its costs in fixed tips, so the preset trades less, banks a part early, and lets the rest of a winner run. Your ticket and your mode are never touched.

  • Fees smart fees on, priority ceiling 0.0002 SOL, slippage ceiling 12%
  • Entry score 68+, 8 buyers, +2 SOL net, 25+ holders, band 20 to 150 SOL, pool 2000 USD+, only a coin up 4 to 35% in 5 min, priced at the bonding curve
  • Exit 70% sold at +18%, the rest rides to +130% or until the trailing stop gives back 45% of its own peak (arm +12%, floor 8 points), hard stop 9%, hold 240 s
  • No break-even order it sells at your own cost of doing business; the trailing stop is what protects a winner
  • Patience a take profit must hold 400 ms before it sells, so one spike is not a fill; a stop or a trail acts in 150 ms
  • Risk 1 coin at a time, 10 buys a minute, pause 8 min after 5 losses, session cap 8 tickets

Beginner Guide

Everything you need to know about Pump.fun in two minutes.

What is Pump.fun?

Pump.fun is a site where anyone can launch a meme coin on Solana in seconds. Thousands launch every day. Most go to zero, a few go up very fast.

What does this bot do?

It watches every new coin on Pump.fun and every trade it can see. It checks how many real people are buying, how fast the price moves, whether one wallet is faking volume and whether the creator dumped. If a coin scores high, it buys a small amount and sells automatically for profit or to cut losses.

Start safely

  1. Stay in Practice Mode and watch a few trades.
  2. Create your wallet in the Wallet tab and back up your key.
  3. Deposit a small amount of SOL.
  4. Switch to Real Money with the Balanced style.

Key words

SOL
The coin used to pay for everything on Solana.
Market cap
The total value of a coin. Low means early and risky.
Take profit
Sell automatically after a gain.
Stop loss
Sell automatically after a loss, so it cannot get worse.
Slippage
The most price movement you accept while buying or selling. Smart fees keeps it as tight as the coin allows.
Priority fee
A small tip that makes your transaction faster. Smart fees follows the live network price so you never overpay.
Private key
The secret that controls your wallet. Never share it.

Be honest with yourself about risk

Most meme coins lose value. Fees (about 1.5% per side) and fast price swings mean the bot can lose money even when it works as designed. Past results do not predict the future. Only use money you can afford to lose.
